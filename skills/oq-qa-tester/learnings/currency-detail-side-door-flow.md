**Trigger:** Testing the sales-channel assignment checkboxes on the legacy Currency detail page (`/admin/edit-currency/?id=<currencyId>`, section "Visibility on sales channels"), e.g. the module-off lock `UpdateCurrencyValidator::validateAssignmentsUnchanged`.

**Rule:** Checkboxes are named `assignedSalesChannels.<salesChannelId>` (channels where the currency is primary are checked + disabled). Save posts via AJAX to `adminUpdateAction` and the page re-renders through `adminIndexAction`: a rejection shows as a red notifier at the top with the raw key, the DB stays unchanged, and the re-rendered form KEEPS the rejected tick, so reload (`navigate` with `force: true` and a cache-buster) before the unchanged-save control. The success notifier ("The record has been saved successfully.") can be missed by a 3 s wait; arm `read_network_requests` (it only records after its first call) or read the Tracy bar's AJAX rows for the `adminUpdateAction` request.
