**Trigger:** Driving a synthetic `*.test` host whose response is a redirect, and a `computer:screenshot` / `read_page` fails with "Permission denied for this action on this domain", or `navigate` fails with "Navigation to this domain is not allowed".

**Rule:** The Claude-in-Chrome allowlist is host(+path)-scoped: `fenix.myshoptet.com.test` and other `*.myshoptet.com.test` storefront paths pass, `www.shoptet.cz.test` (the local InactiveProjectRouter landing) and `/admin/…` on non-fenix hosts are denied (observed 2026-09-07). The navigation itself still happens — only the tool on the landing origin is refused — so take the landing URL + title from `tabs_context_mcp` as evidence, and screenshot only once the tab sits on an allowed host. Never put the screenshot inside a `browser_batch` right after a cross-host redirect: when a later item errors, the captured image is dropped from the transcript ("Image omitted due to error") and cannot be recovered by extract_screenshots.py. `read_network_requests` also loses the pre-redirect document request after a cross-host hop — capture status/Location with `curl -sk -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code} %{redirect_url}'` as the header-level companion evidence, and fall back to curl (not retries) for the admin-runtime control when the `/admin/` navigation is refused.

**2026-09-09 update:** Per-host, not wildcard: `multishop.myshoptet.com.test` (admin and storefront) is allowed while `rofl.myshoptet.com.test` is refused, so "other `*.myshoptet.com.test` pass" above is not reliable. A `computer:screenshot` placed in the same `browser_batch` right after a cross-host `navigate` can be refused while the tab is still on the redirecting host even when the landing host is allowed; take the screenshot as a standalone call after `tabs_context_mcp` shows the landing URL.
