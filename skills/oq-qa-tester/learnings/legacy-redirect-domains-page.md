**Trigger:** Driving the legacy old-admin domain redirects page (`/admin/presmerovani-domen/` list, RedirectDomainsController; add form modal = RedirectDomainController, slug `presmerovani-domen-detail`).

**Rule:** Three traps. (1) Add validation requires a host WITH a subdomain — bare `example.cz` is rejected with "Prosím použijte plnou doménu obsahující i subdoménu"; always seed `www.*` hosts. (2) The row delete anchors (`a.csrf-post-js.delete-item-big`, `data-testid=buttonUniversalConfirm`, hidden until row hover) open a NATIVE `confirm()` despite the "universal" testid — a real click freezes CDP (Input.dispatchMouseEvent timeout). Recover with a `navigate` call (discards the dialog = cancel, delete NOT executed); drive it safely with `window.confirm=()=>true` then a JS `.click()` on the anchor — the csrf-post-js handler does the POST and the success notifier confirms. (3) Add/delete are full POST+redirect actions (notifier "Záznam byl v pořádku uložen." / "…úspěšně odstraněn."), no fetch — a fetch hook captures nothing here.
